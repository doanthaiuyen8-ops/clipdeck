Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ClipDeck is published by p2xstudio. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: ClipDeck does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What ClipDeck accesses

  • The P2 cards and folders you open, so it can list, play, copy, move and delete your clips. Operations happen only on your computer and only when you start them.
  • A small settings file (your folder list, view and language choices) stored in your Windows user profile.
  • Thumbnail images it creates from your clips, cached in your Windows user profile so the app opens faster. They never leave your PC.

What ClipDeck does not do

  • No account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • No network connection: the app does not upload or download anything. Links in the About window open in your browser only when you click them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling ClipDeck removes its settings and thumbnail cache. Your clips are never changed unless you choose an operation.
